Alessio Frosini

Postdoctoral Associate at Yale School of Public Health

I study causal inference and microeconometrics, focusing on the analysis of treatment effects under interference in both experimental and observational settings.

Before joining Yale, I earned my PhD in Economics and Finance from the University of Rome Tor Vergata.
